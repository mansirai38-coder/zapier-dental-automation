# Business Use Case

## Who this is for

Independent dental clinics and small multi-chair practices that:
- Rely on a receptionist or office manager to manually track appointments
- Don't have (or don't want the cost of) a full practice-management platform
- Already use Google Workspace (Forms, Sheets, Calendar, Gmail) day to day
- Want to reduce no-shows and improve patient communication without hiring extra staff

## The problem, in numbers (industry context)

No-show rates at dental and medical practices commonly run in the 5–30% range depending on reminder practices — and manual reminder calls are time-consuming and inconsistent. A significant share of that can be recovered with reliable, automatic reminders and structured follow-up.

## What changes with this automation

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| Staff manually logs each appointment | Form submission auto-populates the patient sheet |
| Reminders sent inconsistently (or not at all) | Reminder email sent automatically, exactly 24h before every appointment |
| New patients get the same generic message as returning ones | New patients automatically receive a dedicated welcome email |
| Follow-up and review requests are an afterthought | Automatically triggered ~1 day after a visit is marked Completed |
| No consistent record of what's been sent | Reminder/Follow-up/Review Request status columns update themselves |

## Why Zapier specifically

- No custom backend or hosting required — the clinic doesn't need a developer on staff to keep it running
- Every tool involved (Forms, Sheets, Calendar, Gmail) is one most clinics already have via Google Workspace
- Changes to the workflow (e.g. editing an email's wording) can be made by a non-developer directly in Zapier's UI

## Constraints acknowledged

- Zapier's free trial only supports multi-step Zaps for a limited time; a paid plan (Starter/Professional) is required to keep this running long-term.
- This automation handles communication and record-keeping — it does not replace a full practice-management or billing system.
- Because this project used Zapier's built-in email step (Gmail), sending volume is subject to standard Gmail sending limits; a clinic with very high appointment volume may eventually want a dedicated transactional email provider.
